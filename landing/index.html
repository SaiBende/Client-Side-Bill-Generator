🪟 Desktop App for Windows

Fully offline invoicing on your own PC

Prefer a real application on your machine? The desktop app runs completely offline. All your invoices live in a local SQLite database in your own Documents folder — no cloud, no tracking, nothing leaves your PC unless you share a link.

  • 100% offline — perfect for shops and workshops with patchy internet 📴
  • Local SQLite database at Documents\BillingApp

💬 FAQ

Common questions

Is ShareMyBill really free? 💯

Yes. Both the web app and the desktop app are free forever — no plans, no trials, no invoice limits.

Do I need an account to create invoices? 🔑

No. You can create and export invoices without any account. An optional free account adds cloud storage and sharing between devices.

Where is my data stored? 🗄️

On the web, drafts and data you don't sync stay in your own browser. If you sign in, your invoices sync to a secure Supabase database, while the desktop app lets you avoid that entirely by storing everything in a local SQLite file in your Documents folder.

What's the difference between the web app and the desktop app? 🌐 vs 🪟

The web app runs in your browser with optional cloud sync. The Windows desktop app runs fully offline and keeps all data on your own computer. Both share the same invoice editor, GST slabs and PDF export.

Does it support GST and Indian formats? 🇮🇳

Yes — GST 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, CGST/SGST split, amount-in-words in Indian numbering (lakh/crore) and a GSTIN-friendly invoice layout.

Can I add my logo and business details? 🎨

Yes. Add your logo image and save your business defaults, so every new invoice already has your brand on it.

How do I contact the developer? 📬

ShareMyBill is built by Sai Bende. Reach out via GitHub, or see the project at Client-Side-Bill-Generator.
